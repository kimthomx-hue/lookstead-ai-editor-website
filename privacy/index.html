Privacy Policy

Summary: How Lookstead AI Editor handles videos, overlays, accounts and exports.

Last updated: 2 November 2026. Contact: privacy@lookstead.com

Overview

Lookstead AI Editor lets you import videos, trim clips, add photo overlays, and export results on your device. When you sign in, we use Supabase authentication (Apple, Google, or email magic links). We do not sell your personal data.

What we collect

  • Videos and images you choose to import for editing (stored on your device unless you upload to a future cloud feature).
  • Onboarding preferences and project metadata on your device.
  • Authentication identifiers if you create an account.
  • Coarse technical data (app version, device type) to diagnose crashes.

How we use data

  • To provide editing, export, and account features you request.
  • To improve reliability and security.
  • Future AI features may process media on secure servers — we will update this policy before they launch.

Retention & deletion

Delete local data in Profile → Delete all data on this device. Signed-in users may delete cloud account data where the delete-account service is configured.

Contact

Questions: privacy@lookstead.com. See also our terms of service and help centre.
