How it works

  1. Upload a video from Photos or Files on your iPhone.
  2. Trim the best section with the visual timeline.
  3. Add photo overlays using the built-in crop editor.
  4. Pick a format for TikTok, Reels or Shorts.
  5. Export to your photo library (development or store build).
