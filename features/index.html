Features

  • Trim & cut

    Set in and out points on a thumbnail timeline, then export the selected clip.

  • Vertical formatting

    Preview 9:16, 1:1, 4:5 and 16:9 frames before you export.

  • Photo overlays

    Crop images with pinch, drag handles and rotation, then place them on your video.

  • Clip suggestions

    Coming soon. Long-form uploads analysed for hooks and highlight moments.

  • Auto captions

    Coming soon. Word-level captions with creator-friendly styles.

  • YouTube import

    Coming soon. The app validates links today; download is not enabled yet.
